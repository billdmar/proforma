Splunk Customer Letter

 

Subject: Splunk Joins Forces with Cisco

 

[Dear NAME // INSERT CUSTOMARY GREETING],

 

I’m reaching out to share exciting news about the future of Splunk.

 

Today, we announced that Splunk is joining forces with Cisco to create one of the world’s largest software companies. This transaction is a transformative milestone that will enable us to accelerate our innovation in security and observability and bring to market best-in-class technologies that protect, connect and advance the missions of organizations and communities all over the world.

 

Splunk has built its business around our fundamental customer promise – to stay ahead of your organization's needs in order to deliver products and offerings that keep your mission-critical systems resilient. This transaction will allow us to build on this promise by combining with a trusted, long-time partner that shares our passion for innovation and customer experience. Together, we will be able to integrate and build on Splunk and Cisco’s industry-leading solutions to deliver comprehensive visibility and insight in the market across security, observability and network operations, powered by AI. We will also have an expanded set of resources to increase investments to accelerate innovation and develop new solutions, as well as broader global reach to support our customers of all sizes, around the world.

 

The resilience of digital systems matters to organizations worldwide now more than ever, and together Splunk and Cisco will provide organizations with the most comprehensive product portfolio needed to help keep their digital systems safe and performant. With Cisco, we will be able to help you access, analyze and act on your data faster and more securely than ever before, empowering you to keep your organization resilient.

 

In terms of next steps, there are no changes to how we do business with you at this time. We expect to complete the transaction with Cisco by the end of the third quarter of calendar year 2024 and will continue to operate as an independent company until that time. It’s business as usual, and we remain focused on serving your organization’s needs and delivering the exceptional customer service that sets Splunk apart. If you have any further questions, please don’t hesitate to reach out to your usual contact at Splunk.

 

Thank you for your continued partnership. We hope you share our excitement about the opportunities ahead.

 

Sincerely,
